# Mindlatch Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026

Mindlatch is an Android app by Gabpet Webcraft. It reminds you of things and can lock your phone
to essential apps for a time you choose. This policy explains what information the app uses and
where it goes.

## The short version

- Your reminders, schedules and settings stay on your phone.
- Mindlatch has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.
- We do not sell or share your information, because we do not receive it.

## Information stored on your phone

Mindlatch saves the following on your device so that it can work:

- The reminders, to-dos and celebrations you create, including their titles, notes, dates and times.
- Your focus lock schedules and the apps you chose to keep available during a lock.
- Your settings, such as light or dark mode.

None of this is sent to Gabpet Webcraft or to anyone else. It is removed when you uninstall the
app or clear its data. If Android's own backup is switched on for your phone, Android may include
this data in the backup it keeps in your Google account. That backup is controlled by your phone's
settings and by Google, not by Mindlatch.

## Permissions and how they are used

**Accessibility service (Focus lock).** If you turn on Focus lock, Mindlatch uses an accessibility
service to enforce the locks you start. While a lock is running, it sees the name of the app that
has come to the front, and covers that app if it is not one you allowed. It cannot read what is on
your screen, what you type, your passwords or your messages, and it does nothing when no lock is
running. Nothing it sees is saved or sent anywhere.

**Installed apps.** Mindlatch reads the list of apps on your phone so you can pick which ones stay
available during a lock, and to recognise your phone, messages and contacts apps. The list is not
saved or sent anywhere.

**Calendar.** If you choose to connect your calendar, Mindlatch reads your upcoming events to show
them in the app, and adds or updates events for reminders you choose to put in your calendar. It
works with the calendar already on your phone. Any syncing with Google Calendar is done by your
phone and your Google account, not by Mindlatch.

**Notifications, alarms and Do Not Disturb.** These are used to alert you at the times you set, and,
if you allow it, to silence other apps during a lock. Your previous setting is put back when the
lock ends.

**Installing updates.** Mindlatch can download a newer version of itself and hand it to Android to
install. Android asks you to confirm every install.

Mindlatch does not ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts, call history or text
messages.

## When the app uses the internet

Mindlatch connects to the internet in only two situations:

1. **Checking for updates.** The app asks GitHub whether a newer version exists, and downloads it
   from GitHub if you choose to update. As with any website, GitHub receives your phone's IP address
   when this happens. No information about you or your reminders is included. See
   [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
   You can turn off automatic checks in Settings.
2. **The Feedback screen.** This shows a Google Form inside the app. It only loads when you open
   that screen. Whatever you type and submit there is sent to Gabpet Webcraft through Google, and
   [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies. Please do not include
   passwords or other sensitive details in feedback.

## Children

Mindlatch is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
As described above, the app does not collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated and the
effective date at the top will change. The latest version is always on this page.
